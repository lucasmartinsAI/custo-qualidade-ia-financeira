Agora, vamos calcular a Duration de Macaulay:

\[
\text{Duration} = \frac{\sum_{t=1}^{n} t \times \text{PV}_t}{\sum_{t=1}^{n} \text{PV}_t}
\]

Substituindo os valores:

\[
\text{Duration} = \frac{\sum_{t=1}^{36} t \times \text{PV}_t}{\sum_{t=1}^{36} \text{PV}_t}

\[
\text{Duration} = \frac{\sum_{t=1}^{36} t \times \frac{860}{(1.04)^t}}{\sum_{t=1}^{36} \frac{860}{(1.04)^t}}
\]

\[
\text{Duration} = \frac{\sum_{t=1}^{36} t \times 860 \times \frac{1}{(1.04)^t}}{\sum_{t=1}^{36} 860 \times \frac{1}{(1.04)^t}}

\[
\text{Duration} = \frac{\sum_{t=1}^{36} t \times 860 \times \frac{1}{(1.04)^t}}{860 \times \sum_{t=1}^{36} \frac{1}{(1.04)^t}}

\[
\text{Duration} = \frac{\sum_{t=1}^{36} t \times \frac{1}{(1.04)^t}}{\sum_{t=1}^{36} \frac{1}{(1.04)^t}}
\]

Agora, precisamos calcular os dois sumatórios:

\[
